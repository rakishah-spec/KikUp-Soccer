## Description
KikUp Soccer helps young players train on their own and get better every day.

Pick your age group (4–8, 9–12 or 13+) and your level, and KikUp gives you drills with goals that match. Prop up your phone, tap Start, and the camera counts your reps for you. Toe taps, wall passes, juggling, shots and more.

TRAIN EVERY PART OF YOUR GAME
• General Training: passing, shooting and dribbling
• Skill moves, from the sole stop to the rainbow flick
• Endurance workouts with spoken cues
• Position training for goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards

THE CAMERA COUNTS FOR YOU
• Counts reps as you go, so you can focus on the drill
• Gives coaching tips when you finish
• Everything happens on your phone. Video is never recorded or uploaded.

STAY MOTIVATED
• Earn up to 3 stars on every drill
• Build a daily streak, with optional reminders
• Track your progress over time

TRAIN WITH FRIENDS (optional)
• Add friends with a friend code
• Keep friend streaks going together
• Friends leaderboard and challenges
• No chat. Parents set up accounts for kids under 13.

Every drill has an animated video to show you how.

## App Review notes
Camera drills use the camera only on the device to count reps; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
To try a camera drill: open any drill marked "Camera counts", tap Start drill, then Start.
Friends features need an account: use the test login provided. Google sign-in is hidden in the app.
