Quick help

  • The camera isn't counting right: stand the phone up straight at about knee height, 3–4 big steps away, so your feet and the ball are in the picture. Good light helps a lot.
  • The camera won't turn on: allow camera access for KikUp in your phone's settings, and close any other app that's using the camera.
  • Need more time to get ready: open the menu, go to Settings → Camera and pick a longer countdown.
  • Deleting an account: open the menu and go to Friends → Account → Delete account.
