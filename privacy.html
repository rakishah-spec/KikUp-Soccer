KikUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: KikUp works without an account, and then nothing leaves your device. The camera counts reps on your phone, and video is never recorded or uploaded. If you turn on Friends, KikUp stores your username or nickname and your training stats so the friends you approve can see your stars and streaks. There are no ads, no tracking, no chat, and nothing is sold.

1. Without an account

You can use every drill without an account. Your level, stars, streak and training history are saved only in your browser on your device. You can clear them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

2. The camera

Camera drills use your phone's camera to find your body and the ball and count your reps. This happens entirely on your device. KikUp never records, saves or uploads video or photos, and no one else can see your camera. The camera is only on during a camera drill, and you can say no to camera access at any time (you can still use the drills that don't need it).

3. Friends accounts (optional)

If you create a Friends account, KikUp stores the following using Google Firebase, a service run by Google:

Friends are added only with a friend code shared in person. Players can't search for each other, and there's no chat or free-text messaging.

4. Children under 13

Children under 13 can't create their own account. A parent or guardian creates a parent account, gives consent, and adds the child as a player with a generated nickname, so no real names are needed. We don't ask children for their name, email, photo, location or any other contact information. Parents can see and delete their child's data at any time (see below).

5. What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.

6. Deleting your data

In KikUp, open the menu, go to Friends → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes the account, every player on it, their stats, friends and challenges. To delete data saved on your device without an account, clear the site's data in your browser or uninstall the app.

7. Where data is stored

Friends data is stored by Google Firebase on servers in the United States, and is protected by security rules so each account can only reach its own data and the stats its friends share. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For questions or requests about your data, contact the KikUp developer at the email address shown on KikUp's Google Play store listing.
