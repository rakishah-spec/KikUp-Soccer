              FAQs

              Getting started

              What is KikUp?

              KikUp is a soccer training app for players to practice on their own. Pick your age group and skill level, and you get drills with goals that match. Many drills use your phone's camera to count your reps for you.

              Is KikUp free?

              Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

              Do I need an account?

              No. Every drill works without an account, and your progress is saved on your device. You only need an account for Friends: friend streaks, the leaderboard and challenges.

              Which age groups is it for?

              Little Kickers (4–8), Juniors (9–12) and Teens (13+), each with Beginner, Developing and Advanced levels. You can change yours any time in Settings.

              Camera counting

              How does the camera count my reps?

              The camera finds your feet and the ball and counts each rep as you go. It all happens on your phone: video is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

              How do I start a camera drill?

              Tap Start drill, set your phone up, then tap Start. You get a countdown to get into position, and the camera starts counting when it ends.

              Where should I put my phone?

              Stand it up straight at about knee height, 3–4 big steps away, so your feet and the ball are in the picture. Your whole body is fine too. Some drills tell you a better spot on the camera screen.

              The camera isn't counting right. What can I do?

              Check the phone is upright and not tilted down, the ball is in the picture the whole time, and there's good light. If it's still off, turn on Help fix camera counting in Settings → Camera, do the drill, enter your real count and send the test data through the form below.

              The camera won't turn on.

              Allow camera access for KikUp in your phone's settings, close any other app using the camera, then tap Try the camera again.

              Can I get more time before it starts counting?

              Yes. Pick a countdown from 3 to 20 seconds in Settings → Camera. You can also choose the front or back camera there.

              Stars, streaks and progress

              How do stars work?

              Each drill gives up to 3 stars: 3 for reaching the goal, 2 for getting close, and 1 for finishing it. Your best result for each drill and level is kept.

              How does my streak work?

              Train at least one drill a day to keep your streak going. Miss a whole day and it starts again from 1.

              Where can I see my progress?

              Open the menu and tap My progress to see your stars, streaks and the days you trained.

              Will I lose my progress?

              Without an account, progress is saved on your device, so clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes it. With a Friends account, it's backed up and syncs to your other devices.

              Friends and accounts

              How do I add friends?

              Each player has a friend code. Share it in person, and your friend enters it on the Friends page. Players can't search for each other.

              How do friend streaks work?

              A friend streak grows on every day you and your friend both train. If one of you misses a day, it resets.

              Can kids under 13 use Friends?

              Yes, but a parent or guardian creates the account and adds the child as a player with a made-up nickname. Teens 13 and up can make their own account.

              Is there chat?

              No. Friends can see each other's stars and streaks and send challenges with preset messages, but there's no chat or free-text messaging.

              How do I delete my account?

              Go to Friends → Account → Delete account. This deletes the account and all its players, stats, friends and challenges.

              Still need help?

              Found a bug, the camera counted wrong, or have an idea for a new drill? Tap below to fill in a quick form.
